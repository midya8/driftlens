04 / A FINITE CHECK, NOT A SAMPLING PROMISE

Count the rejections.

Each dark vertical marker is the supplied alpha; bars show rejection fractions on a shared 0-1 scale.

These are exact fractions of this supplied orbit, not measured false-positive rates across real datasets, estimates of power, or evidence that the null is true. At every supplied alpha, ranking the complete tied global tail cannot reject more than an alpha fraction of a uniformly selected allowed allocation. The experiment must justify that uniformity.

06 / THE EVIDENCE HAS EDGES

Keep the question finite.

The independent native checker replays every allowed allocation, every score, tied rank, global tail and rejection count. It reconstructs the PSD certificate rather than trusting the generator. Replay is not authentication.

A supplied finite family must be fixed independently of observed labels, or selected by a label-invariant rule. Omitting tried kernels, searching blocks, group-dependent preprocessing or repeatedly watching windows changes the question. This inspector does not fix those choices.

No automatic bandwidth choice, new statistical method, strong component-wise multiple-testing inference, conditional or causal drift explanation, practical-effect threshold, deployed-model quality, equivalence or always-valid monitoring claim. The original floating RBF tools remain separate and unchanged.
